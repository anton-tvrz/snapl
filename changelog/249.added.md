The dashboard's history API answers four more series from the audit trail when they are named with `series=` (spec 015): `in_sync_devices` and `open_drifts`, one point per scan, counted over the devices in intent; `runs`, the runs that started in each bucket by trigger and outcome (`schedule.succeeded`, `operator.failed`, and so on); and `repair_duration`, one point per repaired drift with its duration from scan to scan. `/api/v1/history` with no `series=` answers the original four, as before. A new route, `GET /api/v1/drift-summary`, gives each device's drift over a range in one request: its episodes, how many are still open, its time drifted from scan to scan, and the most paths it had missing and undesired. `invoke history.archive` exports it and every history series, and `snapl dashboard --snapshot` carries both, so a recording shows them too. The page has a new panel, Drift by device, after the history: each device that drifted in the page's range, ranked, with a bar of its time drifted, its drifts and how many are still open, and the most paths it had missing and undesired. A row opens the device. Another new panel, Fabric history, sits between the history and drift by device: four small charts over the page's range, of the devices in sync at each scan against the devices in intent now, the open drifts at each scan, the runs per bucket by trigger with failures hatched and running runs outlined, and each repaired drift's duration from scan to scan. Each chart has a text alternative and its numbers in a table.
