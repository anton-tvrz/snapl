The dashboard's history API answers four more series from the audit trail when they are named with `series=` (spec 015): `in_sync_devices` and `open_drifts`, one point per scan, counted over the devices in intent; `runs`, the runs that started in each bucket by trigger and outcome (`schedule.succeeded`, `operator.failed`, and so on); and `repair_duration`, one point per repaired drift with its duration from scan to scan. `/api/v1/history` with no `series=` answers the original four, as before. A new route, `GET /api/v1/drift-summary`, gives each device's drift over a range in one request: its episodes, how many are still open, its time drifted from scan to scan, and the most paths it had missing and undesired. `invoke history.archive` exports it and every history series, and `snapl dashboard --snapshot` carries both, so a recording shows them too. The page has a new panel, Drift by device, after the history: each device that drifted in the page's range, ranked, with a bar of its time drifted, its drifts and how many are still open, and the most paths it had missing and undesired. A row opens the device.
