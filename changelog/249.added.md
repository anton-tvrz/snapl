The dashboard's history API answers four more series from the audit trail when they are named with `series=` (spec 015): `in_sync_devices` and `open_drifts`, one point per scan, counted over the devices in intent; `runs`, the runs that started in each bucket by trigger and outcome (`schedule.succeeded`, `operator.failed`, and so on); and `repair_duration`, one point per repaired drift with its duration from scan to scan. `/api/v1/history` with no `series=` answers the original four, as before.
