The dashboard's look rules are written down and checked (spec 014): spacing in multiples of 8 px, one type scale stepping by 1.25 from 14 px, and panels lifted by a tinted shadow in the light theme. `dev/skills/dashboard/SKILL.md` has them as a "Look" section, and a test fails on new code that breaks them. `node packages/presentation/ui/e2e/look.mjs <url> <dir>` renders the page from fixtures in light and dark and prints its height, so a change can be looked at with no lab; the screenshot script now waits until every panel has loaded. Every panel on the designed page now has one frame: its title on the left, room for one action on the right, and its source and age in a footer; in the light theme a soft shadow lifts it from the page, and in the dark theme its border does. Groups of panels have a small label above them.
