The dashboard's look rules are written down and checked (spec 014): spacing in multiples of 8 px, one type scale stepping by 1.25 from 14 px, and panels lifted by a tinted shadow in the light theme. `dev/skills/dashboard/SKILL.md` has them as a "Look" section, and a test fails on new code that breaks them. `node packages/presentation/ui/e2e/look.mjs <url> <dir>` renders the page from fixtures in light and dark and prints its height, so a change can be looked at with no lab; the screenshot script now waits until every panel has loaded. Every panel on the designed page now has one frame: its title on the left, room for one action on the right, and its source and age in a footer; in the light theme a soft shadow lifts it from the page, and in the dark theme its border does. Groups of panels have a small label above them. The page now leads with four numbers above the fabric: devices in sync (of those in intent), open drifts, the latest repair's duration from scan to scan, and the last 24 hours' deploys by outcome. Each links to the panel with its detail, and a number that isn't known is said in words, never shown as zero. In the drift lanes, devices no longer in intent are folded into one row that gives their count and can be opened (it is open when one of them is selected), and the headline gives the open drifts as a count with a chip per device in intent; drifts on devices that left intent are counted, not listed by id. The hourly history is now a chart: scans, the most drifted devices, deploys and scheduled repairs per bucket, with failures hatched and repairs outlined so nothing depends on colour, and empty buckets left empty. Its table, with every column, is behind "Show the table".
