The dashboard has a new look (spec 014), with no new dependency. The page now leads with four numbers above the fabric: devices in sync, open drifts, the latest repair's duration from scan to scan, and the last 24 hours' deploys by outcome; each links to its detail, and a number that isn't known is said in words, never shown as zero. Beside the fabric, a ring gives the share of devices in sync, above the devices that differ ranked by how many paths differ. The header shows the time the page is showing as a clock that runs when live and stands still in an archive, a recording or at a past time. The drift lanes fold devices no longer in intent into one row, and the headline lists open drifts as a chip per device. The hourly history is a chart of scans, the most drifted devices, deploys and scheduled repairs, with failures hatched and repairs outlined so nothing depends on colour; its table is behind "Show the table". Each metrics panel leads with its latest value, a rate under one a second reads per minute (0.024/s is 1.4/min), and the metrics sit right after the history under a time range that stays on screen as they scroll. A run with no recorded steps shows its six blocks as one row of chips. Every panel shares one frame (title, one action, source and age in the footer) and is grouped under a small label, and the page no longer scrolls sideways in a 360 px window. The rules behind the look (spacing in multiples of 8 px, one type scale stepping by 1.25 from 14 px, a tinted shadow in the light theme) are in the "Look" section of `dev/skills/dashboard/SKILL.md`, and `src/look.test.ts` fails on code that breaks them. `node packages/presentation/ui/e2e/look.mjs <url> <dir>` renders the page from fixtures in light and dark with no lab, and the screenshot script now waits until every panel has loaded.
