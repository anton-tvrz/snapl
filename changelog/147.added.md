The worker now serves Prometheus metrics (spec 012). The Temporal SDK's runtime exposes its own workflow and activity metrics and `snapl_gnmi_call_duration`, a histogram in milliseconds of every gNMI call `apply_config` and `collect_drift_state` make, labelled by `device`, `use_case`, `operation` (`configure` or `collect`) and `outcome` (`ok` or `error`). The endpoint is `http://127.0.0.1:18091/metrics`, moved with `WORKER_METRICS_PORT` and `WORKER_METRICS_BIND`, and `WORKER_METRICS_PORT=off` turns it off. A taken port logs a warning and the worker starts without metrics. The dashboard settings gain `PROMETHEUS_ADDRESS` (default `http://localhost:${PROMETHEUS_PORT:-18090}`) for the metrics store that a later PR adds.

The dashboard reads the metrics store (spec 012). `GET /api/v1/metrics/{view}` answers one of six named views (`workflow-duration`, `gnmi-duration`, `gnmi-errors`, `worker-lag`, `worker-up`, `source-health`) over a range, as series of points with their unit. The request picks the view and the range, never the query. The step is raised when a range would exceed Prometheus's point limit, and the response says so. Answers are cached for one scrape interval (15 s). A Prometheus that is down, or refuses the query, is a view with `available: false` and its reason, never an HTTP error. `GET /api/v1/sources` now lists `prometheus` too, from its `/-/ready` probe.

The dashboard serves its own metrics and shows the measured ones (spec 012). `GET /metrics` gives `snapl_dashboard_source_up` and `snapl_dashboard_source_fetch_duration_milliseconds` for each source the dashboard has read, in Prometheus text format, so Prometheus can record when the audit trail, Infrahub, Temporal, CI, the lab runs or Prometheus itself stopped answering. The page gains a Metrics section with one panel per view, a 1 h / 24 h / 7 d range, line charts for durations and errors, and up/down strips for the worker and the sources. A view that can't be read shows why and what to run instead of a chart.

Prometheus is part of the dev stack (spec 012). `invoke dev.deps` starts `prom/prometheus:v3.10.0` on `127.0.0.1:18090`, scraping the worker (18091) and the dashboard (18030) every 15 s. Its data lives in `.snapl/prometheus` on the host, so `dev.down --volumes` and `demo.reset` keep it. Retention is by size only (`PROMETHEUS_RETENTION_SIZE`, default 2 GB), so history isn't dropped after a fixed number of days.

History can be archived and the metrics store purged (spec 012). `invoke history.archive` writes `.snapl/archives/snapl-history-<time>.tar.gz` (`SNAPL_ARCHIVE_DIR` moves it). It holds a consistent copy of the audit DB, a Prometheus snapshot, the recorded live test runs, a 7-day export of the dashboard's JSON, and a `manifest.json` saying what each part is and where it came from. A part that can't be read is recorded as missing with its reason, the rest is still archived, and the task exits 1. `invoke history.purge` deletes `.snapl/prometheus/` and nothing else. It refuses while Prometheus is running, and asks for `purge` to be typed unless given `--yes`.
