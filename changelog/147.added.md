The worker now serves Prometheus metrics (spec 012). The Temporal SDK's runtime exposes its own workflow and activity metrics and `snapl_gnmi_call_duration`, a histogram in milliseconds of every gNMI call `apply_config` and `collect_drift_state` make, labelled by `device`, `use_case`, `operation` (`configure` or `collect`) and `outcome` (`ok` or `error`). The endpoint is `http://127.0.0.1:18091/metrics`, moved with `WORKER_METRICS_PORT` and `WORKER_METRICS_BIND`, and `WORKER_METRICS_PORT=off` turns it off. A taken port logs a warning and the worker starts without metrics. The dashboard settings gain `PROMETHEUS_ADDRESS` (default `http://localhost:${PROMETHEUS_PORT:-18090}`) for the metrics store that a later PR adds.

The dashboard reads the metrics store (spec 012). `GET /api/v1/metrics/{view}` answers one of six named views (`workflow-duration`, `gnmi-duration`, `gnmi-errors`, `worker-lag`, `worker-up`, `source-health`) over a range, as series of points with their unit. The request picks the view and the range, never the query. The step is raised when a range would exceed Prometheus's point limit, and the response says so. Answers are cached for one scrape interval (15 s). A Prometheus that is down, or refuses the query, is a view with `available: false` and its reason, never an HTTP error. `GET /api/v1/sources` now lists `prometheus` too, from its `/-/ready` probe.
