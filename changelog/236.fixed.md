The dashboard headline no longer counts drift on devices that have left intent as open. After a re-seed the old device IDs are never scanned again, so their last drifted scan left episodes that could not close, and the headline read "11 drifts open" with every fabric device in sync. Those episodes are now counted apart: "No drift open. 11 episodes never closed on devices no longer in intent." The API is unchanged: the episodes are still reported as open.
