`snapl remove <device> --apply` removes the config a device carries that intent never asked for (spec 013, US2). It shows the preview, asks for confirmation (or takes `--yes`; with no terminal, `--yes` is required), then starts a `RemoveConfig` workflow under `remove-config-<device id>`, so only one removal of a device runs at a time. The workflow re-scans and writes nothing if the deletes differ from the ones confirmed, or if a deploy to the device is running; otherwise it sends every delete in one gNMI Set of deletes only, which the device applies whole or not at all, and verifies with a fresh scan that nothing undesired is left under a delete and nothing intent manages went missing. The executor checks each path against its own guard before it dials. The confirming operator, the confirmed paths, the device's response and the verification are recorded in the audit trail. Scheduled runs never remove config.
