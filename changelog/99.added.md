Drift scans now run on a schedule (spec 009). Each use case can have one Temporal Schedule (`drift-response-<use case>`, every 5 minutes by default) that starts a `DriftResponse` workflow: a `ScanDrift` child, then the schedule's response. In `report` mode, the default and the only mode a schedule is created in, it records what drifted and writes nothing. In `remediate` mode, switched on per use case with a confirmed `snapl schedule mode remediate`, it reconciles exactly the devices with forward drift; devices with only undesired config, or that could not be read, are excluded and recorded. `invoke demo.up` creates the report-only schedule and `demo.check` fails without one. New `snapl schedule create|show|mode|pause|resume|trigger|delete` commands work with no worker running, and every schedule change is audited with its actor. Overlapping runs are skipped, and missed firings are never replayed (catch-up window of one interval).
