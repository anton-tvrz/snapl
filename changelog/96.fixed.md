The dcfabric seed now sets each device's `lab_node_name` to its static containerlab management address (`172.20.21.x`) instead of a `clab-dcfabric-*` container hostname. Those hostnames only resolve inside the docker network, so a freshly seeded SoT produced a fabric where every gNMI apply and collect failed to dial until the addresses were patched into Infrahub by hand. A unit test now fails if the seed's dial targets and the lab topology's static pins drift apart.
