The Presentation block is no longer a stub: `snapl` is a real operator CLI over the closed loop. `snapl deploy <device>` runs a verified deploy, `snapl scan --use-case <uc>` reports per-device drift with the exact differing paths, `snapl reconcile [devices|--drifted]` heals it behind a confirmation prompt, `snapl audit --workflow|--device` reads the durable trail, and `snapl status` reports whether Temporal, the SoT and the worker are actually healthy. Device names resolve to ids automatically, `--json` gives every command machine-readable output, and a uniform exit-code contract (0 clean, 1 error, 2 drift) lets a script tell "drifted" from "broken" without parsing anything. Anticipated failures — unreachable Temporal, unknown device, no worker polling, missing audit database — render as one actionable line rather than a traceback.
