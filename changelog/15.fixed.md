Fix a SoT-missing device being counted as *failed* instead of *skipped* by `ReconcileDevicesWorkflow` (spec FR-004). `DeployIntentWorkflow` now maps a non-retryable `IntentNotFoundError` from the intent fetch to `reason=DEVICE_NOT_FOUND` — the signal reconcile's skip path consumes but which was never produced — while transient SoT failures (e.g. connection errors) still terminate with `INTENT_UNAVAILABLE` and count as failed.
