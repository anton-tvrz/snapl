A scheduled repair that fails on any device now switches its drift schedule back to `report`. The reason (the run and the devices) becomes the schedule's note and is recorded in the audit trail as `stood_down`, by `snapl:stand-down`. Scans keep running; nothing is paused. Before, a device that rejected its intent got one failed repair per interval until an operator stepped in. A repair that does not complete at all stands the schedule down too. A device skipped because a deploy was in flight, or a run an operator cancelled, does not count as a failure. This is spec 009's deferred FR-018, built as its research R8 designed it.

A scheduled repair no longer pushes a bad intent edit to the whole fabric. When a `remediate` run finds forward drift on more than half of the devices it scanned, and on more than one, it repairs nothing. It switches the schedule back to `report` with a note such as `<run>: 3 of 4 devices drifted, more than half; repaired nothing`. `snapl audit` shows each held-back device as `decision=blast_radius`. Devices with only undesired config don't count; errored ones count in the total. The threshold is fixed at one half (spec 018).
