A scheduled repair that fails on any device now switches its drift schedule back to `report`. The reason (the run and the devices) becomes the schedule's note and is recorded in the audit trail as `stood_down`, by `snapl:stand-down`. Scans keep running; nothing is paused. Before, a device that rejected its intent got one failed repair per interval until an operator stepped in. A repair that does not complete at all stands the schedule down too. A device skipped because a deploy was in flight, or a run an operator cancelled, does not count as a failure. This is spec 009's deferred FR-018, built as its research R8 designed it. There is still no guard against many devices drifting at once.
