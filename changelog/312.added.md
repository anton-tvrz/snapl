The collector can stream a device's telemetry (spec 016). `GnmiCollector.stream_telemetry` holds one sample-mode gNMI subscription per device, every 10 s, for only what intent names: each interface's state and counters (octets, errors, discards), each BGP session's state and prefixes received, and the device's CPU and memory. It yields vendor-neutral samples, and when the stream fails, or stays silent for longer than the dial timeout plus three intervals, it ends by raising `TelemetryStreamError`. The parser is checked by replaying subscriptions captured from leaf-01 and spine-01. Nothing runs it yet: the worker starts the streams in a later PR.
The Observer can judge a device's health from intent and its telemetry: `healthy`, `unhealthy` with one reason per fault ("ethernet-1/49 down (expected up)", "BGP 10.10.1.1 active (expected established)"), or `unknown` when no telemetry has arrived for three intervals. An interface or session must be out of its expected state for longer than one interval before it counts, and an item intent names that the device never reports counts as `absent`. CPU and memory never change health, and health never changes drift.
The worker can export eight new metric families on its existing endpoint: `snapl_interface_up`, `snapl_interface_octets_total`, `snapl_interface_errors_total`, `snapl_interface_discards_total` (each with a `direction` of `in` or `out`), `snapl_bgp_session_established`, `snapl_bgp_prefixes_received`, `snapl_device_cpu_percent` and `snapl_device_memory_percent`. They are labelled by device, use case, and the interface or peer intent names, and nothing else. The device's counters are totals; the worker adds only what each total grew by, starting from 0 and treating a fall as a restarted counter, so a rate never goes negative or spikes. The audit trail has a new event, `health_changed`, which records a device's health before and after, with its reasons.
