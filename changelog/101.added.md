The audit trail now records which way each drift runs and who started each deploy. A scan's per-device record adds `undesired_paths`, the paths that are on the device but not in intent. A deploy's start record adds `parent_workflow_id`: the reconcile that started it, or `null` for an operator's `snapl deploy`. Both fields are additive, and the spec 010 dashboard reads them to show drift direction and run triggers from history without guessing.

`snapl dashboard` serves a local, read-only page and JSON API (`/api/v1/devices`) showing every device's role, drift state, last scan and last deploy, read from the audit trail and the Source of Truth. When either is down, the page says which one and what to run.

The dashboard also serves `/api/v1/runs` and `/api/v1/schedule`: recent workflow runs with their outcome and what triggered them (the schedule or an operator), what is running now, the drift schedule's state and who paused it, devices whose scheduled repair has failed three times in a row, and whether a worker is polling.

The dashboard also serves `/api/v1/history`: drifted devices and undesired paths per scan, and deploys and scheduled repairs per hour, day or week, over any range of the audit trail. Hours with no activity show as gaps, not zeros, and reconciles and remediating runs are marked.
