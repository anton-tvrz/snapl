Add NAF Orchestrator building block — Temporal-based durable workflows that compose Intent, Executor, Collector, and Observability into the closed NAF loop. Includes `DeployIntentWorkflow` (single-device fetch → apply → collect → verify → audit), `ScanDriftWorkflow` (fabric-wide drift evaluation), `ReconcileDevicesWorkflow` (operator-initiated remediation via child workflows), and the durable, append-only `SqliteAuditLog` that takes over the persistence obligation deferred by the Observability block's in-memory audit log.
