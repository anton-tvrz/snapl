Every drift schedule command names the Temporal it talks to (#391). `snapl schedule create`, `show` and `mode` print a `temporal` row with its host and namespace (and `--json` carries `temporal: {host, namespace}`), the confirmation before switching to remediate or triggering a remediate run names it, and `snapl status` shows the namespace beside the host. A mistyped or leftover `TEMPORAL_HOST` now shows before an unattended schedule lands on the wrong Temporal. Nothing is refused.
