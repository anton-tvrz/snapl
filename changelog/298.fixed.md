The Temporal cancellation tests can no longer hang CI. A run that stalls after its cancel now fails within 20 s, naming its status, its pending activities and its last history events. A stuck worker or test server shutdown fails naming which one. The held activity is released by the test, rather than after a 30 s sleep. A test that still exceeds pytest's 300 s timeout now dumps every thread and ends the run, where before the job ran until it was cancelled.
