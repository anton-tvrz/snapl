Removals show in the record (spec 013, US3). `snapl audit` prints a removal's end as `removed N: <paths>; verified`, or why it wrote nothing, with the operator who confirmed it. On the dashboard, a drift episode that a removal closed says it was repaired by an operator's removal. A device with undesired config shows `snapl remove <device>` to copy, beside `snapl reconcile <device>` when intent is missing too, and its drawer shows the latest removal preview and the entities it left in place. A removal run shows who confirmed it, what it deleted, the device's response and what the verifying scan found, or why it wrote nothing. Both dashboard pages only show removals; neither can start one.
